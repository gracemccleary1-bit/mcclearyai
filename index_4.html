Why it hasn’t happened yet

You need tailored solutions that slot into how you already operate

You're probably reading all about how AI is disrupting companies and industries, but how can you leverage it in a way that makes sense for you?

Which tools

There are all sorts of personal assistant apps, but many of them require you to move your data into their systems and servers. They also don't operate in the apps you're comfortable in. To use them, you have to adopt an entirely new app or website.

What goes where

You should be able to decide what information gets automated versus what information gets hyper-intelligence digital organization.

How it’s wired

Many personal AI assistants are sold as an open box magical solution: you download something and your life improves. The real work lies in connecting the accounts, deciding what the system knows, routing which questions go where, making it run without being asked.

The short list

What I will never install

I do not work for any sort of AI company. I have no loyalty to any given product, solution set or app. This means I will only recommend what genuinely will offer you the fixes you're looking for.

  • Nothing that listens. No ambient recorders, no always-on microphones, no meeting bots.
  • Nothing that wants your attention. If it messages you, something needed you.
  • Nothing that talks to your children. These are systems for the adults running a household.
  • Nothing that sends on its own. Every email, message and payment is drafted and waits for you.
  • Nothing that keeps your data. It lives in accounts you own and can close — never inside a company whose terms I can’t explain to you.

It's your data, your systems, but better.
